# Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

## Feedback by email

If you choose **Send Feedback** in the app, FamFinance opens your device's email app with a draft addressed to `longnguyen95.xn@gmail.com`. The app does not send the email automatically, attach app data, or transmit feedback to a FamFinance server. You can review, edit, or discard the draft.

If you send the email, the recipient will receive the email address associated with your email account and the message and any information you choose to include. Feedback is used to respond to you and improve the app. Email is handled by your email provider and the recipient's email provider under their own privacy practices. Feedback messages are retained only as long as needed to respond and maintain support records.

This feedback feature does not automatically collect or send your expenses, income, notes, contacts, device identifiers, or other app data.
